about this project

I built this because I kept walking into the voting booth knowing nothing about half the people on the ballot. It's a simple community-scored tracker for all 528 members of the 119th US Congress. No accounts, no server, no ads. Data lives in your localStorage.

scoring guide

metric 0 = 100 =
voting recordnever keeps promisesalways votes as promised
campaign financeopaque / PAC-heavytransparent / small-dollar
scandal scoremany controversiesclean record
lobbyingheavy donor capturevotes independently

rate limiting

5 votes per hour, 1 per member, tracked via browser fingerprint. It's a speed bump not a wall — clearing localStorage resets it, which is fine.
